# How to Play: Overworld

## About this guide
This guide operates under the assumption that you know the basics of Minecraft, the movement, basic mechanics, etc. For a comprehensive guide for complete beginners to the game, see [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vcRoRl-CQys) youtube video, or the thousands of other guides on the internet. It would also be recommend that you spent a good amount of time playing the game casually before trying to speedrun, to allow yourself to hone important skills like inventory management and higher level movement.

## Speedrun Basics
Beating Minecraft quickly quickly consists on the broadest level of 4 things:

- Building a nether portal(often shorthanded to your "enter").
- Finding a bastion and trading with piglins, obtaining string, ender pearls, and obsidian.
- Finding a fortress and obtaining at least six(6) blaze rods.
- Finding the stronghold.
- Entering the End and killing the dragon with explosives.

## Overworld Types

In MCSR ranked runs, the overworld that you get is preditermined and can be of one of five types:
- [Village](./village) (most beginner friendly)
- [Shipwreck](./shipwreck)
- [Desert Temple](./desert_temple)
- [Ruined Portal](./ruined_portal)
- [Buried Treasure](./buried_treasure)(1200+ elo only)

  The type determines what you do for each overworld. But in general for beginner runs three things are essential, food, an iron pickaxe, and either a fire charge or a flint and steel. Food is pretty self explanatory but an iron pick is needed to break gold blocks in the bastion, and the flint n' steel is used to light the nether portal.
